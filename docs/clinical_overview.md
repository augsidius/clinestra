# Clinical use

This section is intended for clinicians, nurses and clinical leads working with an application built on Clinestra. Screens vary between applications, but the rules described here apply to all of them.

## What Clinestra does

Clinestra runs long-term care as well as recording it. It tracks who is in a programme, what is due, what was missed and how a patient is progressing. It enforces the rules around that care: who may do what, what needs consent, and what a person has to decide. A patient stays on the programme version they enrolled under when a new version is published.

## What it does not do

Clinestra does not replace clinical judgement. It does not make diagnoses or prescribing decisions. Any clinical content your organisation loads must be reviewed by your own clinicians, and your organisation remains responsible for its regulatory position.

## Who can do what

| Decision | Who |
|---|---|
| Record a diagnosis, activate a careplan, prescribe | A clinician |
| Open a visit, complete tasks, log observations | The care team |
| Log diaries and complete questionnaires | The patient, for their own record |
| Approve a note sent for review | Someone other than the requester |
| Review clinical templates | A clinical lead or administrator, who did not create them |

Your organisation may give some people narrower access.
