# Safeguards

These rules are enforced by the system, whichever app you use.

## The record

- Finalized notes cannot be changed. Corrections are added as addenda, so the original stays visible.
- A note cannot be finalized until its required fields are complete.
- A clinical record finalizes only when all its pages are finalized.
- Clinical data is not deleted. Records entered by mistake are marked as entered in error.
- A patient has one current clinical state in general and one per condition. Earlier states are kept.

## Accountability

- Every change records who made it and when.
- The history is linked so that tampering can be detected, and your administrator can verify it.
- Nobody can approve their own request.
- Patient queues can be forced past a step only with a reason, and the forced move stays visible.

## Access

- Data is separated between sites.
- Patients see only their own records and those of people they are an active guardian or care taker for.
- Patients see documents only when they are ready and shareable.

## Limits you should know

These safeguards protect the record and the process. They do not check whether a clinical decision is correct. A score, alert or template is only as good as the content your organisation has reviewed.
