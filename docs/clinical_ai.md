# AI in clinical care

AI agents may read the record, prepare drafts and submit proposals. They cannot change the clinical record without a clinician's decision.

## What AI may do

- Read the record of a patient who has given AI consent
- Draft notes and documents for you to check
- Propose a change, with its reasoning

## What AI may not do

- Work on a patient without an active `ai` consent
- Finalize a note or sign a document
- Record a diagnosis, start a plan or prescribe by itself

## Reviewing a proposal

Each proposal shows the change, where it came from and why it was suggested. You can:

- **Accept** it. The change is recorded under your name.
- **Accept with edits.** Correct it first.
- **Reject** it, with a reason.

Nothing changes in the chart until you accept. Verify each proposal against the source record before accepting it. AI output can be incorrect or incomplete, including when it is expressed with confidence.

## AI-drafted notes

A drafted page is marked as an AI draft. Read all of it before you sign, because finalizing makes the content yours and locks the page.

## Automatic rules

Your organisation can set rules that respond to events, such as a high score. Administrative effects, like creating a call task, can happen automatically. Clinical effects always become proposals for a clinician.
