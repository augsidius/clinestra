# Consent

Consent is recorded separately for each purpose.

| Scope | Covers |
|---|---|
| Treatment | Care being provided |
| Data sharing | Releasing documents and information |
| AI | AI access to the patient's record |
| Procedure | A specific procedure |
| Communication | Contacting the patient |
| Research | Use in research |

## How it works

A consent starts as requested. It can then be granted or refused, and a granted consent can be withdrawn or can expire. Actions that need consent are refused without an active one.

Withdrawing a consent is recorded and stops further actions that need it. It does not erase what was properly done before.

## Guardians

A guardian can consent for a patient only in the scopes the patient's relationship record allows.

## Signed forms

A consent form can be sent to a patient through a signing link, which can be protected by a one-time code, or signed in person. Forms can list who must sign: the patient, the clinician and a witness.

Your organisation decides which forms to use, and should have their wording reviewed for your jurisdiction.
